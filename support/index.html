Setting up your shift pattern

Set a shift pattern to fill your calendar. With WorkLife Pro, open Settings → Automatic alarms to choose an exact clock and working shift types. Automatic alarm dates follow the current schedule; removing a matching shift removes its automatic occurrence. Personal routine clocks and standalone alarms remain independent. Existing saved alarms keep their scheduling mode unless you choose Follow current shift schedule and save. Cancelling conversion preserves the original alarm. If Today says “No pattern set”, tap Set up just above it, or follow the step-by-step pattern guide. It explains ready-made and custom rotations.

My alarm didn't ring

  • Check the upcoming preview: select at least one working shift type and choose Previous day, Shift-start day or Next day relative to the date the shift starts. Only matching working shifts create occurrences. A previous- or next-day occurrence can fall on an off day. Review permissions and delivery status on the alarms list; a preview alone does not confirm delivery.
  • Notifications: check iPhone Settings → WorkLife → Notifications is on.
  • Ring on Silent: turn on Ring as real alarms in WorkLife so shift alarms ring like the Clock app's alarms, even on Silent, and allow Alarms when your iPhone asks.
  • Focus: shift alarms are marked Time Sensitive so they can come through a Focus. If you use a Focus, check that it allows Time Sensitive notifications.
  • Open WorkLife at least once a week: alarms are set up ahead of time, and the Alarms screen shows the date they are set through. Opening the app extends them.

Free trial and WorkLife Pro

The free version of WorkLife keeps working for as long as you like: your calendar, a saved rotation and your own alarms. WorkLife Pro adds automatic shift alarms, the money and wellbeing tools, extra rotations, calendar sync and iCloud backup. Eligible subscribers can receive a one-month free trial; eligibility is shared between the monthly and annual plans. Open Settings → Plan → Upgrade in WorkLife. Apple shows your localized price and any trial before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Installing WorkLife alone does not start a trial.

Restore a purchase or use Pro on a new iPhone

WorkLife → Settings → Plan → Restore Purchases. This needs an internet connection and the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WorkLife, or WorkLife → Settings → Plan → Manage Subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Cancelling renewal keeps Pro active until the current subscription period ends. After expiry, Free features remain available and your saved data is kept.

Move your data to a new iPhone

  • iCloud Backup (part of WorkLife Pro): on your old iPhone, WorkLife → Settings → iCloud Backup → Back up now. On the new iPhone, sign in to the same Apple Account, then WorkLife → Settings → iCloud Backup → Restore from iCloud. Restoring is always available, with or without a subscription.
  • Backup file: WorkLife → Settings → Back up your data creates an encrypted file protected by your passphrase. On the new iPhone, use Restore from a backup and enter the same passphrase. (Export data is different: it makes readable spreadsheets and calendar files for other apps, and cannot be restored into WorkLife.)
  • Moving to a new iPhone with Apple's own transfer or an iPhone backup also brings WorkLife's data with it.

Delete your data

Everything is on your iPhone, so you are in control of it. See Keeping and deleting your data in the privacy policy for each step, including your iCloud Backup copy.
