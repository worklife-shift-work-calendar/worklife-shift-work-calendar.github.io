Privacy Policy

WorkLife: Shift Work Calendar for iPhone · Effective date: 23 September 2026

The short version. WorkLife keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We, the developer, cannot see your shifts, your pay, your spending or anything else you put in the app.

Your information leaves your iPhone only if you choose to send it somewhere: an export you save, iCloud Backup if you turn it on, something you share, or your iPhone's own backups.

What we collect

Nothing. WorkLife has no server. Everything you enter — shift patterns, pay settings, spending, leave, habits, alarms, checklists, certificates — is stored in a database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Your iPhone's own backups

Like every app, WorkLife's data is included in your iPhone's own backups if you use them — an iCloud backup of your iPhone, or a backup to a computer. Those backups are made and kept by Apple and by you, not by us.

Backups and exports you make in WorkLife

Encrypted backup file

Back up your data in Settings creates a full backup file. It is encrypted on your iPhone with a passphrase only you know (AES-GCM, using Apple's CryptoKit), and it goes wherever you choose to send it. We never receive a copy, and we cannot recover your passphrase — nobody can open the file without it.

Readable exports

Export data in Settings creates ordinary files you can open in other apps: a spreadsheet (.csv) of your hours and pay, a spreadsheet of your spending, and your roster as a calendar file (.ics) or as text. These files are not encrypted — anyone who has the file can read it. They go only where you choose to send them, and we never receive a copy.

Automatic snapshot

WorkLife keeps a recent copy of your data on your iPhone so you can undo a mistake. It stays on your iPhone.

iCloud Backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Back up to iCloud, WorkLife saves a copy of your data to your own private iCloud storage, using the Apple Account already signed in on your iPhone. With it the app saves the time of the backup and the type of device it came from (for example “iPhone”). There is no WorkLife account and no server of ours: the copy is stored by Apple, encrypted in iCloud, counts against your iCloud storage, and is reachable only through your Apple Account — we have no access to it. Restoring it is always available, with or without a subscription. Turn the feature off at any time and nothing further is uploaded.

Photos of your training certificates are not included in the exported file or in iCloud Backup. They stay on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups).

Permissions the app may ask for

Each one is optional, asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it, and used for nothing else. You can change any of them at any time in the iPhone Settings app → WorkLife.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets and Live Activities

WorkLife offers actions you can use with Siri and the Shortcuts app, makes some of your items (shifts, checklist items, training records, habits, and the names — never the amounts — of your spending entries) findable in Spotlight search on your iPhone, and can show your next shift in widgets and Live Activities. All of this works on your iPhone. When you speak to Siri, Apple handles your voice under Apple's own privacy policy; WorkLife receives only the request itself. Widgets read information the app shares with them on your iPhone. If you turn on Require Face ID, WorkLife removes its items from Spotlight and stops suggesting your recent actions to Siri until you turn it off.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, WorkLife can use Apple's on-device language model to turn a rota you describe in words into a shift pattern, and to suggest a category for a spending entry. This runs entirely on your iPhone: nothing is sent to WorkLife, to Apple or to anyone else, and you review the result before it is saved. On other iPhones the app uses its own built-in rules instead.

Sharing

WorkLife sends nothing anywhere on its own. When you choose to share, you pick what and where:

  • Share this month creates a picture of your calendar.
  • Sharing a shift pattern (as a QR code or a text code) includes the pattern's name and its shift types — their names, start and end times, break, colour and symbol. It never includes your pay, your spending or any dates.

Diagnostics

If something goes wrong inside the app, WorkLife writes a small technical log on your iPhone. It leaves your iPhone only if you tap Share diagnostics in Settings and choose where to send it. It records where in the app the problem happened, the type of error, the time and the app version — not your shifts, pay, spending or anything else you entered.

Purchases and subscriptions

WorkLife Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold by Apple. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, card or payment details. The app asks Apple only whether you have an active WorkLife Pro subscription and when it renews or ends. Any free trial is Apple's own subscription offer.

To manage or cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone: WorkLife → Settings → Plan → Restore Purchases.

Advertising and tracking

WorkLife shows no advertising and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If a future version ever changed this, this policy would be updated at this address before that version is released, and any consent the law requires would be asked for first.

Third-party code

Apart from Apple's own system frameworks, the only outside code in WorkLife is GRDB, an open-source database library that runs entirely on your iPhone.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep none of your data, because we never receive any. You stay in control of every copy:

  • Single items — delete them in the app at any time.
  • Everything on your iPhone — delete the WorkLife app. This removes its database, certificate photos and automatic snapshot from your iPhone.
  • Your iCloud Backup copy — turn off Back up to iCloud in WorkLife, then go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage, choose WorkLife and delete its data.
  • Backup files and exports — delete them wherever you saved or sent them.
  • Your iPhone's own backups — managed in iPhone Settings or on your computer.
  • Permissions — withdraw any of them in iPhone Settings → WorkLife (Apple Health: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → WorkLife).

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing we can delete for you — but you are always welcome to ask us about it.

Children

WorkLife is a shift-work planning tool made for working adults. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included, because it collects no data at all.
