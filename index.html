WorkLife: Shift Work Calendar

For iPhone

Built for rotating shifts: your rota, your pay and your wake-up alarms in one app.

No WorkLife account. No ads. No tracking. Core planning works offline. Data is stored on your iPhone, with optional backup to your private iCloud storage.

  • Your rota, filled in. Set your shift pattern once and your calendar fills itself.
  • Your pay, worked out. With WorkLife Pro, see what you've earned from the shifts you actually worked.
  • Independent alarm times. Your own alarms are free. With WorkLife Pro, save repeating wake-up and leave-by shift alarms. Work-pattern edits do not move or cancel saved alarms; edit or disable them separately.

Getting started

The first minute in WorkLife sets up your rota and your alarms: Getting started walks through the setup, and How to set up your shift pattern covers the pattern builder in full.
